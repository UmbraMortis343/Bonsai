# Bonsai: notes for Claude

Read this first in every session. It replaces the long design conversation the project grew out of.

## What it is
A knowledge-retention app. You build knowledge graphs of ideas, grouped into topics, and keep them alive by reviewing them. The home screen is a garden of floating voxel islands: each topic is a biome that grows as ideas are added and dries out when ideas wait too long to be reviewed.

- Live: https://umbramortis343.github.io/Bonsai/ (installable web app, laptop and phone)
- Repo: https://github.com/UmbraMortis343/Bonsai (public, AGPL-3.0-or-later from 0.6.0)
- Owner: Karthik (GitHub UmbraMortis343). Use the handle, not his name, in anything public.

## Words
Hub → island → topic → knowledge graph → idea. He sees a graph as ideas strung together rather than a trunk and branches (said 2026-11-15); the knowledge view redesign should drop those words. In code a topic is still a `zone`, a knowledge graph a `tree` and an idea a `node` (and CSS classes `lbl zone`, `lbl tree`, `lbl trunk` for the first idea); nothing on screen says zone, tree, trunk or branch any more (`run18-kview` checks the menus), bar the tech tree's own Tree mode.

## How he wants it
- Very little text in the app. No explanatory copy, labels only where needed.
- Every action reachable by keyboard, with its key shown on screen (back arrows show Esc; Shift+Esc goes straight back, `S.backTo`, 0.18.2). Phone gets a touch compromise.
- Replies short and plain. Show pictures of visual work; he judges looks himself.
- He decides direction. Ask before changing something he designed; just fix bugs.
- Look (from 2026-11-15): one palette of white, black and an earth green, with warm greys and an earth ochre for "needs water"; no slate or navy blues. Rectangles with slightly rounded corners (3–6px), never pills or circles. Bars are docked, not floating: a full-width top bar (`--top`), side panels full height against an edge, a full-width bottom bar; the canvases shrink to the space left (`--dl`/`--dr` on `body`). The islands, tech tree, knowledge map and meaning view keep their own colours.

## Controls (0.27.0, his revamp: few rules, the same everywhere)
- Mouse: click picks (a second tap opens on touch), double-click goes in or opens, right-click gives the thing's menu (the tech tree keeps his right-click: open or close what's under it), drag moves what you hold (an idea in a graph or on the board) and otherwise turns or pans, wheel zooms. `stage.onDouble`, `stage.onMenu` (a right-click that didn't drag); `ideaItems`, `graphItems`, `linkMenu`.
- Keys: 1–9 and arrows pick, ↵ goes in (in a graph or on the board ↵ adds beside, Tab under; ⇧↵ opens the board's graph in 3D, or a watered graph), Esc lets go then steps out, ⇧Esc straight back, 0 fits, V the other view everywhere (board/3D, separate/joined, tree/web; in a graph, the same graph on the board, `openOnBoard`), ⇧Tab windows (from the garden, Knowledge opens on what's picked; in the garden plain Tab does it too), [ ] side panels, / search, I capture, K tech tree, O notes beside a graph, ? the grouped list (`shortcuts`). One meaning per letter: G is only Graft (and Grow on a note page), W pen page, Y Today, Shift+V vocab, Shift+A actions.
- Gone: Move mode (dragging an idea moves it; Put back is in its menu), the WASD fly keys (`FLY` is empty), F fly-to (double-click an idea), G to open things, J and L and B for views (all V).
- Links: on the board hover and click pick a link (`onPickLink`, `S.glink`, foot with Label R and Delete link Del), right-click gives its menu; the card and the idea panel list links with ×; deleting a link has Undo (`removeLink`). `run27-controls`.

## Getting around and Today (0.28.0, 0.35.0)
- Fewest clicks between places (his ask, 0.28.0). / opens the go-to box everywhere (`find`, `places`): islands, topics, graphs, ideas, notes in one list, meaning matches added when the model is on; Enter goes there (`toGarden({ isle, topic })` for garden places, the board for a graph, `jump` for an idea). The path over a board is buttons into the garden.
- Watering is in the background (his call, 0.35.0): no water buttons, no W, no Watering tab. Review happens through Today (Y, `src/today.js`): the day's 10 questions, the streak and the month. `water(ids, treeId)` and the `#review` round stay underneath (Today, orals and Sunday use them; `bonsai.water` for tests). A topic dries only after ideas wait long past due (focus.js `dry`: 21 days Focus, 42 Keep, never Settled). Thirsty (H) is off by default (`S.thirsty`, pref `thirsty`).

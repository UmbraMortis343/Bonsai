# Bonsai: notes for Claude

Read this first in every session. It replaces the long design conversation the project grew out of.

## What it is
A knowledge-retention app. You build knowledge graphs of ideas, grouped into topics, and keep them alive by reviewing them. The home screen is a garden of floating voxel islands: each topic is a biome that grows as ideas are added and dries out when ideas wait too long to be reviewed.

- Live: https://umbramortis343.github.io/Bonsai/ (installable web app, laptop and phone)
- Repo: https://github.com/UmbraMortis343/Bonsai (public, AGPL-3.0-or-later from 0.6.0)
- Owner: Karthik (GitHub UmbraMortis343). Use the handle, not his name, in anything public.

## Words
Hub → island → topic → knowledge graph → idea. He sees a graph as ideas strung together rather than a trunk and branches (said 2026-11-15); the knowledge view redesign should drop those words. In code a topic is still a `zone`, a knowledge graph a `tree` and an idea a `node` (and CSS classes `lbl zone`, `lbl tree`, `lbl trunk` for the first idea); nothing on screen says zone, tree, trunk or branch any more (`run18-kview` checks the menus), bar the tech tree's own Tree mode.

## How he wants it
- Very little text in the app. No explanatory copy, labels only where needed.
- Every action reachable by keyboard, with its key shown on screen. Phone gets a touch compromise.
- Replies short and plain. Show pictures of visual work; he judges looks himself.
- He decides direction. Ask before changing something he designed; just fix bugs.
- Look (from 2026-11-15): one palette of white, black and an earth green, with warm greys and an earth ochre for "needs water"; no slate or navy blues. Rectangles with slightly rounded corners (3–6px), never pills or circles. Bars are docked, not floating: a full-width top bar (`--top`), side panels full height against an edge, a full-width bottom bar; the canvases shrink to the space left (`--dl`/`--dr` on `body`). The islands, tech tree, knowledge map and meaning view keep their own colours.

Growth = ideas in the topic / 150 (a "full plot"), shaped by a power of 0.6, capped at 2. Health = share of reviewable ideas not due.

The garden panel (docked right, hidden and shown from the top bar) starts with the name of what's picked (back arrow, click the name to rename it, ⋯ only for what's left: moving a topic, new or remove island), then day/night, detail and the actions (Tech tree opens on that island's tier, the topic picked), then the lists, then that thing's settings at the foot (biome and landmark; centrepiece, figure, mountain); garden totals sit in the top bar; Knowledge spread folds away (remembered). G in the garden (and Knowledge graphs in its panel) opens the knowledge view showing only what's picked: `#/forest/<hub, island or topic id>` (`scopeOf`, `S.fscope`), with Show all. Garden levels: every hub → one hub → one island → one topic (`S.hub`, `S.isle`, `S.topic`; a garden with one hub skips the hub step). An island holds six topics. An island has a mountain if its centrepiece is the arches or its `mountain` flag is set (island menu); it rises on the first topic's plot, and without the arches' river its waterfall lands in a small lake at its foot. Water never lies under a centrepiece: `isles-world.js` measures each centrepiece's reach per direction from the blocks it lays (`spanOf`, `keepAt`) and places ponds, the lake and the stream around it; `tests/run13-water.mjs` checks this over many islands. Use `keepAt` for anything new that carves or floods ground. The map is the only place hubs and islands are made once `mapAt` is set: Import map (menu) takes the text the Bonsai Map page copies. The map wins for what is on it; a topic moved by hand stays until it moves on the map. A topic with no ideas is under mist (only when `mapAt` is set), an empty hub is a mist bank. Split (S, at 200%): merges the topic's graphs under one trunk, then its largest main branches (up to five) become topics on a new island, each linked back to the trunk.

## Keeping sessions cheap
One card per session. Start by reading this file and the card. Don't name a class `empty`: `#empty` is the empty-garden box (it was `.empty` and caught an emptied note line once). Don't re-read whole large files when a section will do; avoid loading pictures you don't need. End the session after the release and update the board.

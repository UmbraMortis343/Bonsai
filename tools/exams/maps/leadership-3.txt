graph: Strategy
Strategy | Choosing where to play and how to win, and what not to do
  Vision and mission | Where you are going, and what you do to get there
  Diagnosis | An honest account of the situation and the main challenge
    SWOT analysis | Strengths, weaknesses, opportunities and threats
  Strategic choices | A few priorities that rule other things out
    Trade-offs | Saying no to good ideas so the best ones get resourced
    ! Set priorities with half the budget | Name the goal, rank the bets, cut whole items rather than thinning all of them
  OKRs | Objectives with measurable key results, set each cycle
  Cynefin | Match the approach to the problem: clear, complicated, complex or chaotic

graph: Culture
Culture | What people actually do when nobody is checking
  Values in practice | Values are shown by what gets rewarded, tolerated and punished
  Leading by example | People copy what leaders do before what they say
  Servant leadership | The leader exists to serve the team's growth and success
  Hiring and onboarding | Who joins and how they start shapes the culture most
  Rituals and stories | Repeated practices and retold moments carry the culture
  Ethics | Doing the right thing when it costs something
    ! Respond to a values breach by a star performer | Act on the behaviour, apply the same standard, explain why to the team

graph: Leading change
Leading change | Moving a group from one way of working to another
  Kotter's eight steps | From creating urgency and a guiding coalition to anchoring the change
    Creating urgency | Making the case that staying the same is the bigger risk
    Short-term wins | Early visible results that build belief
  Change curve | People move through shock, resistance and exploration before commitment
  Resistance | Usually a signal of loss, fear or missing information
    ! Announce an unpopular change | Say what, why and what stays the same; listen; repeat it often
  Communicating change | Far more often than feels necessary, through several channels

graph: Stakeholders and influence
Influence | Getting things done through people you don't manage
  Stakeholder mapping | Plot people by interest and power to decide how to involve them
    ! Win over a sceptical sponsor | Find what they care about, show the risk handled, ask for a small first step
  Negotiation | Reaching an agreement that both sides can keep
    Interests over positions | Ask why they want it; there is often more than one way to meet it
    BATNA | Your best alternative if no agreement is reached
  Managing up | Giving your own leaders what they need to back you
  Building networks | Relationships made before you need them
  Persuasion | Credibility, evidence and emotion together

graph: Leading leaders
Leading leaders | Getting results through other people's teams
  Organisation design | Structure follows strategy: who reports where and who decides what
    Span of control | How many people one leader can support well
  Developing leaders | Growing people who can run teams without you
    Succession planning | Knowing who could step into each key role
    ! Pick between two candidates to lead a team | Weigh how they get results through others, not only their own output
  Decision making at scale | Push decisions to where the information is
    Reversible and irreversible decisions | Move fast on the first kind, slowly on the second
  Crisis leadership | Be visible, decide on what you know, communicate often, review after
  Measuring what matters | A few indicators that show whether the strategy is working
